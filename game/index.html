SOLMAR BAY

VICE COAST STORIES

Rico Vega is back in Solmar Bay. Steal cars, dodge the law, run five missions and take the town — on foot and behind the wheel, under a sun that never quits.

SOLMAR BAY — an original open-world crime game · keyboard + mouse · saves locally

HOW TO PLAY

W A S D / Arrows — move on foot · drive when in a car
Mouse — aim the pistol · Click — shoot
E — steal / enter a car · exit a car
Shift — run · Space — handbrake
P / Esc — pause · M — mute
Follow the ◆ yellow marker. Crimes raise your wanted stars — break line of sight and stay clean to cool off. Getting busted or wasted costs $200.
